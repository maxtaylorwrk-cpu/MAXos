# CONTINUITY_PROTOCOL

Purpose

This protocol describes the minimal, durable layers a future AI or developer should recover to reconstruct Max OS and the Lola relationship, even if the original chat history is missing.

Continuity layers (four)

1) Identity
- Who Max is: values, character, long-term mission, identity signals, preferred styles of collaboration.
- How Lola should work with Max in light of identity and values.

2) Knowledge
- Approved principles, decisions, frameworks, lessons, and durable insights that drive choices.
- These are the items that should persist as "permanent knowledge" (see knowledge_items).

3) History
- Past conversations and artifacts showing how thinking evolved and why certain decisions were made.
- Helpful for understanding context but not required to operate.

4) Current State
- What Max and Lola are actively working on now: recent decisions, open actions, unresolved issues, and next steps.

Recommended recovery order for a future session
1. Lola Constitution (docs/LOLA_CONSTITUTION.md)
2. Max identity/context (explicit identity files, bios, mission statements)
3. Lola operating instructions and behaviour constraints
4. Approved permanent knowledge (knowledge_items)
5. Current-state handoff (open actions, project status, sprint-style notes)
6. Relevant recent history (conversations, journals, messages)

Why this order
- Identity and operating rules ground the behaviour of the model and protect continuity of values.
- Approved knowledge and current state give the model the durable rules and the immediate context it needs to act.
- History is useful but less critical than the other layers and may be bulkier to restore.
